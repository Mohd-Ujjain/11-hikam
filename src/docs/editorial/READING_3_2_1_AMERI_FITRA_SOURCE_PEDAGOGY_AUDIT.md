## Aid audit and new structure

Prior chapter's five main-body tables were mostly repeated *«وجه النظر / بيانه في القراءة»* displays, with subjects presented as points instead of the genuine logical operation each learner needs.

- **§1:** one genuine side-by-side table comparing presential/acquired knowledge across common axes; separate short teaching tip for the four introductory questions
- **§2:** seven consecutive argument premises made independently visible in the existing hierarchy component; a memory-aid retrieval bridge
- **§3:** genuine comparison between original disposition and the author's rejected idea of fully acquired explicit infant belief; a mistake box guarding the distinction and a dedicated source-faithful definition
- **§4:** source-based hierarchy grouping experiential testimony, childhood evidence, adult research and explanatory hypotheses by their argumentative function; concept relationship between observation, proposed mechanism and inference
- **§5:** hierarchy distinguishing dramatic example, first Kant premise and second Kant premise, followed by checkpoint on source boundary

The established chapter concept, important source quotation, source-adjacent disclosures, accompanying analogy, concept relations, high-yield exercise, the prior final checkpoint and **all six independent revision sections** are retained. All five established contextual Companion cues and Source Depth entries stay in their source paragraphs without editing the original manifest, passage text or citations. Table headers are marked `scope="col"` and `scope="row"` for accessibility.
