## Why this needs a distinct editorial design

This is a collection of **several authorial perspectives**, not a primary source with a single governing argument. Kellenberger's critical treatment of deductive proofs and evil is distinct from Khosrowpanah's origin-of-religion framework and Imam's metaphysical investigation. A generic “Arguments for and against God” table would collapse those distinctions. In particular, the first two English extracts are most directly connected to subtopics **3.2** (arguments for God) and **3.5** (evil), while the Arabic origin-of-religion reading is directly connected to **3.1.4–3.1.5**; cross-references must not replace their independent treatment.

The source's likely role is **additional reading**, not evidence that a sixth authored dossier already exists. There are presently no three 3.1.6 Total Recall guides in the corresponding Google Drive 3.1 folder. Do not invent them or set an arbitrary Companion quota.
