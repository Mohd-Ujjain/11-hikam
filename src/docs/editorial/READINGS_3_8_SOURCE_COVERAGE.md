## 3.8.2 — النظريّات في نسبة العلم والدين

**Attribution:** مصطفى عزيزي، *العلم والدين*، «النظريّات في نسبة العلم والدين .. عرضٌ ونقدٌ». The [publisher’s catalogue](https://aldaleel-inst.com/633/العلم-والدين) identifies the author and book; the parent institution’s [published author extract](https://imamhussain.org/arabic/42047) independently matches the Independence discussion. These references corroborate attribution only; the assigned sources control scope.

**Assigned scope:** The prepared Arabic Word and its paired 38-leaf scan begin at the unnumbered chapter opener, followed by the uninterrupted printed sequence 32–68. The last leaf ends during the first general objection to Independence, at the text’s mention of individual and social dimensions. The opener names four positions, but the extract treats only conflict and independence; no later treatment of overlap, mutual support, or the final preferred position is imported. Mutual support appears only where the assigned conflict critique itself mentions it. All three matching recalls informed structure only; none supplied new claims or extended the excerpt.
