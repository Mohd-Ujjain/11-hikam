## Verification per reading

1. Read the original file's actual title page/heading; identify all credited authors and original publication title.
2. Compare the title exactly to the chapter `h1.chapter-title` and the `span.tt` of the matching TOC entry; update them only against verified source evidence.
3. Display the source author on the title page (existing `.chapter-source`) and in the TOC source-credit field (`.ts`), without rewriting other chapters.
4. Rebuild `index.html`, `src/philosophy_study_notes.html` and `service-worker.js`; run the applicable verification suite and confirm the Pages deployment.
5. Change the row status here to “Verified” and record the primary filename/edition when available.
