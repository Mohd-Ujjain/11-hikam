## Corrections and preservation

- The generic God/world distinction is not a claim of necessary existence; the reference to what “most monotheists” believe was removed. Both the true and false halves of NN are visible without opening a disclosure.
- Greek and Hindu world-models are both beginningless, but differ in necessity and creative dependence. Weak providence is defined in terms of how public events arise, not merely as a synonym for karma.
- Distinguish Yandell’s proposed neutral definition of religion from his substantive judgements about particular positions. The Jain/Theravāda opposition makes the logical conflict concrete without assuming in advance which claim is true. Yandell explicitly rejects the characterized Religious Pluralism that treats straightforward doctrines as false or meaningless and assesses the traditions only against the Pluralist’s moral ideal. **His claim is that this form is self-refuting and self-contradictory**; do not soften that assertion or expand it into an unsourced refutation of every meaning of pluralism. His criticism of treating religious propositions as private unassessable feelings and refusal of argument as a matter of “politeness” is likewise presented as his own explicit authorial judgment.
